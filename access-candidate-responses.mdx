You can review each candidate's responses, scores and grading status. Where you do it depends on the quiz platform you use.

## For Socratease Quizzes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Results Page">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Results** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Candidate">
    The **Quiz score** column shows each candidate's score, with a link under it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/access-candidate-responses/quiz-score-column.png" alt="Quiz score column with View answers under one score and Grade quiz under another" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Scores and Responses">
    The link under the score shows one of two states:

    | Link | What It Means | Action |
    |---|---|---|
    | **View answers** | The attempt has been graded | Click **View answers** to see the candidate's full responses |
    | **Grade quiz** | The candidate submitted the test and it has not been fully graded | Click **Grade quiz** to review and grade their responses |

    Either link opens the candidate's report on the **Results** tab.

    <Frame>
      <img className="border rounded-xl max-w-xs" src="/images/results/individual-submission-details.png" alt="A candidate's responses and points scored" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
To find a candidate without opening a test first, go to [**Latest attempts**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/latest-attempts/). Click the percentage in the **Quiz score** column to open that candidate's answers.
</Tip>

## Grading Responses Manually

The Results page of a Socratease quiz gives you three ways to start grading.

| Where | What to click |
|---|---|
| The banner **You have N ungraded submissions** | **Grade now in Socratease** opens the quiz's Individual Analytics |
| The **Ungraded** tile | **Grade now** opens the quiz's Individual Analytics |
| The **Quiz score** column of an attempt | **Grade quiz** opens that candidate's report on the **Results** tab |

To list only the attempts that need grading, click the **Graded** filter above the table and choose **Not graded**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/access-candidate-responses/ungraded-submissions.png" alt="The ungraded submissions banner and the Ungraded tile" />
</Frame>

On a proctored test, if a candidate never submits, click **View report** on their attempt and then **Mark as Finished** to submit it on their behalf. Without proctoring, an unsubmitted attempt cannot be marked as finished. This releases their results according to your [result release setting](/show-results-to-candidates).

## Question Insights and Individual Analytics

Socratease gives you three analytics views for a quiz: Overall Analytics, Question Insights and Individual Analytics. To open them, click **Users' answers** at the top right of the test's Results page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/access-candidate-responses/results-page-links.png" alt="Edit settings, Open quiz and Users' answers at the top of the Results page" />
</Frame>

### Question Insights

Question Insights shows a per-question breakdown of how all candidates answered, with the percentage and count of candidates who picked each option.

<Warning>
Only automatically graded questions appear in Question Insights. Manually graded question types are not included.
</Warning>

### Individual Analytics

Individual Analytics shows one candidate's full submission at a time: points scored, questions answered correctly, submission time, and time taken.

- Use the **pencil icon** next to any question's points to adjust that candidate's score.
- Use **Add Evaluator Feedback** to leave a note on their submission.
- Use **Prioritize Ungraded** to jump straight to submissions that still need manual grading.
- Use **Filter by Result** to show only correct, incorrect, or ungraded responses.
- Use **Download All Responses** to export every candidate's answers.

<Note>
Group By and Filter by Tag are also available here. See [Using Tags](/using-tags) for how to set up and use tags in your results.
</Note>

## For Google Forms

Google Forms keeps its own responses, outside AutoProctor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Responses Tab">
    Open the Google Form you used for the test and click **Responses** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Individual Responses">
    Click **Individual** and use the arrows to move between candidates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details on Google Forms' response features, see the [official Google documentation](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/139706).

<Note>
If you can't see responses in Google Forms, see the guide on [Google Forms responses not visible](/google-forms-response-not-visible). For Microsoft Forms and IFrame tests, see [Where Can I See My Quiz Results?](/where-to-find-quiz-results).
</Note>
