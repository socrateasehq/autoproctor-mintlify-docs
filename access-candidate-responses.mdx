After candidates complete a test, you can review their individual submissions to see detailed responses, scores, and grading status. The process differs depending on which quiz platform you use.

## For Socratease Quizzes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your dashboard">
    Visit your [AutoProctor dashboard](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) and locate the test you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View under Results">
    Click the **View** button under the **Results** section of the relevant test. This opens a list of all candidate submissions (Overall Analytics).

    <Frame caption="Click on View Answers for individual responses">
      <img className="border rounded-xl" src="/images/results/submissions-list.png" alt="List of candidate submissions showing names, scores, and grading status" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check scores and responses">
    Each submission shows one of two states:

    | State | What It Means | Action |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Score link with percentage** | The candidate finished the test and it has been graded | Click the score link to view their full responses |
    | **Blue Grade button** | The candidate has not finished the test, or it has not yet been graded | Click **Grade** to review and grade their responses |

    Clicking either link opens a detailed page showing all responses, points scored, and candidate-specific information.

    <Frame caption="Individual submission detail page with responses and scores">
      <img className="border rounded-xl max-w-xs" src="/images/results/individual-submission-details.png" alt="Individual submission detail page showing candidate responses and points scored" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Grading Responses Manually

Some attempts require manual action before their results become available, for example when a candidate never submits the test themselves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the submission">
    Click **View Report** next to the candidate's attempt to open their response detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the attempt as finished">
    Mark the attempt as finished to submit it on the candidate's behalf. This releases their results according to your [result release setting](/show-results-to-candidates).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Question Insights and Individual Analytics

Beyond Overall Analytics, Socratease gives you two additional analytics views for a quiz.

### Question Insights

Question Insights shows a per-question breakdown of how all candidates answered, with the percentage and count of candidates who picked each option.

<Warning>
Only automatically graded questions appear in Question Insights. Manually graded question types are not included.
</Warning>

{/* TODO: Add screenshot of the Question Insights view */}

### Individual Analytics

Individual Analytics shows one candidate's full submission at a time: points scored, questions answered correctly, submission time, and time taken.

- Use the **pencil icon** next to any question's points to adjust that candidate's score.
- Use **Add Evaluator Feedback** to leave a note on their submission.
- Use **Prioritize Ungraded** to jump straight to submissions that still need manual grading.
- Use **Filter by Result** to show only correct, incorrect, or ungraded responses.
- Use **Download All Responses** to export every candidate's answers.

<Note>
Group By and Filter by Tag are also available here. See [Using Tags](/using-tags) for how to set up and use tags in your results.
</Note>

## For Google Forms

Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, and IFrame tests manage their own response data independently of AutoProctor. For Google Forms specifically, to view individual candidate responses:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Google Form">
    Go to the Google Form you used for the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Responses Tab">
    Select the **Responses** tab at the top of the form editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Individual Responses">
    Click the **Individual** tab to browse through each candidate's responses one at a time. Use the arrow buttons to move between submissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details on Google Forms' response features, see the [official Google documentation](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/139706).

<Note>
If you can't see responses in Google Forms, see the guide on [Google Forms responses not visible](/google-forms-response-not-visible). For Microsoft Forms and IFrame tests, check the Responses section in that platform as you normally would. See [Where Can I See My Quiz Results?](/where-to-find-quiz-results) for an overview of where to find results on each platform.
</Note>
