---

**Unique test URLs** give every candidate their own test link, tied to their email address. Each link works only for that email and only once. By default, AutoProctor sends the candidate a one-time verification code to confirm their identity before the test starts.

<Note>
This feature is available only on the **Elite Plan** and works exclusively with **Socratease Quizzes** and **IFrame Tests**. It is not compatible with Google Forms or Microsoft Forms, as those require platform-specific sign-in. See [Payments and Credits](/payments-and-credits) for plan details.
</Note>

## How to Generate Unique Links

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Access & login Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Access & login** from the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Unique Test URLs">
    Turn on the **Unique test URLs** switch, then click **Save settings**. Links can be generated only after the setting is saved. If your plan does not include the feature, the switch shows an **Elite** badge.

    {/* screenshot: Access & login section of Test Settings for a Socratease test, cropped to the "Unique test URLs" switch turned on and saved, with its hint "Generate one link per candidate. Forces email login." and the "Generate unique links" link below it */}

    <Frame caption="Unique test URLs switched on and saved, with the Generate unique links link below it">
      <img src="/images/invite-candidates-via-email/unique-test-urls-on.png" alt="Unique test URLs switch turned on, with a Learn More link, the hint Generate one link per candidate. Forces email login, and a Generate unique links link underneath" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Unique Test URLs Page">
    In the **Access & login** section, click **Generate unique links** under the switch. You can also click **Get unique links** next to the test link at the top of the Settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Email Addresses">
    On the **Generate** tab, enter the candidates' addresses in **Email recipients**, separated by commas or new lines. To load them from a file, click **Upload CSV**. Only the first column of the file is read, and **Download sample CSV** gives you a template.

    You can add up to 500 addresses in one batch, and each address can appear only once.

    {/* screenshot: Unique test URLs page for a Socratease test, Generate tab, cropped to the "Email recipients (max 500)" box holding three addresses, the "Upload CSV" button, the "Download sample CSV" link, the "Login not required" checkbox unticked and the "Generate 3 links" button */}

    <Frame caption="Three email addresses entered on the Generate tab of Unique test URLs">
      <img src="/images/invite-candidates-via-email/generate-unique-links.png" alt="Email recipients box holding three email addresses, with an Upload CSV button, a Download sample CSV link, an unticked Login not required checkbox and a Generate 3 links button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose How Candidates Sign In (Optional)">
    Leave **Login not required** unticked to make each candidate verify their email. Tick it to log candidates in automatically when they open the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the Links">
    Click **Generate links**. The button shows how many links it will create, for example **Generate 3 links**. The page then opens the **Links** tab with the new links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Links">
    On the **Links** tab, click **Copy link** next to an address, or click **Export** to download every link as a CSV file. Send each candidate their own link by email, your learning management system, or another private channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Links Tab

The **Links** tab lists every unique link generated for the test. You can search by email and filter by **Status** or **Login**.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Email** | The address the link belongs to |
| **Status** | **Not used**, **Started**, or **Submitted** |
| **Login** | **No login** for links generated with **Login not required**. A dash means the candidate verifies their email first |
| **Created** | When the link was generated |
| **Used at** | When the candidate used the link |
| **Link** | The unique link, with a **Copy link** button |

## How Verification Works

How a candidate gets into the test depends on whether you ticked **Login not required** when generating the link.

| Link type | What the candidate does |
|---|---|
| Standard link | Opens the link, then enters a one-time verification code sent to the email address tied to that link |
| **Login not required** link | Is logged in automatically on opening the link. There is no email verification, and these links never expire |

The verification code ensures only the intended recipient can use each link. A link stops working once the candidate has submitted the test. To let a candidate take the test again, generate a new link for the same address.

<Warning>
A **Login not required** link lets anyone who has it start the test as that candidate. Share these links only with the candidate they belong to.
</Warning>

## Important Limitations

| Limitation | Details |
|---|---|
| Common link is preview only | While **Unique test URLs** is on, the shared test link at the top of the Settings page only opens a preview. Only the unique per-candidate links grant access. |
| Email login only | Candidates can sign in only with email. Under **Login providers**, Google and Microsoft are switched off. |
| No revocation | You cannot invalidate sent invitations after they are generated. |
| No free trial | This feature cannot be tested before purchasing the Elite Plan. |
| Batch limit | You can generate at most 500 links at a time. Generate another batch for more candidates. |

<Warning>
Once you save the test with **Unique test URLs** on, the common test link stops working for candidates. Make sure you are ready to distribute the individual links before you save.
</Warning>
