Proctoring settings control what AutoProctor monitors while a candidate takes a test. They live in the **Proctoring** section of a test's **Settings**.

## How to Configure Proctoring

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Proctoring Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Proctoring** from the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn Proctoring On">
    Turn on the switch at the top right of the **Proctoring** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Settings and Save">
    Turn on the checks you need under **What gets tracked**, **Enhanced proctoring** and **Who is informed**. Each group is described below. Then click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If the test sends results to a Google Sheet, saving with proctoring **Off** removes the Sheet link in the **After the test** section. Results are only sent to a Google Sheet for proctored tests.
</Warning>

## What Gets Tracked

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/proctoring-settings/what-gets-tracked.png" alt="The Proctoring section switched on, with the options under What gets tracked" />
</Frame>

| Setting | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Tab switching** | Detects when a candidate switches to a different browser tab or application. Captures a screenshot of the tab they switched to. |
| **Prevent multiple monitors** | Blocks the test while an extra monitor is connected. Works on desktop only, in supported browsers. |
| **Camera** | Detects if no face is visible, or if multiple faces are visible. Captures photos as evidence. |
| **Microphone** | Monitors the sound environment and records audio when noise is detected. |
| **Photos at random** | Captures random photos throughout the test at randomized intervals, for you to review. |
| **Multi-session detection** | Detects attempts made from multiple devices or sessions at the same time. |
| **Enforce full screen** | Forces candidates to take the test in full screen mode. Exiting full screen is recorded as a violation. |
| **Enforce desktop** | Requires a desktop or laptop computer. The test will not open on phones or tablets. |
| **Photo before test start** | Captures a photo of the candidate before the test begins. |

### Customize the photo prompt

When **Photo before test start** is on, a **Customize the photo prompt** switch appears below it. Turn it on to replace the default "Look directly at the camera…" message with your own text, up to 200 characters.

Use this to ask candidates to show their ID card in the photo, for example "Please show your face along with your student ID".

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/proctoring-settings/photo-prompt.png" alt="Customize the photo prompt switched on under Photo before test start" />
</Frame>

<Note>
**Customize the photo prompt** is an **Elite** feature. See [Feature Comparison](/feature-comparison) for plan details.
</Note>

## Enhanced Proctoring

Each enhanced option costs extra credits per attempt, on top of the 1 base credit of a proctored attempt. See [Enhanced Proctoring](/enhanced-proctoring) for the full setup guide.

| Setting | What It Does | Extra Credits per Attempt |
|---|---|---|
| **ID card verification** | Verifies the candidate's identity with a photo of their ID card | 4 |
| **Impersonation detection** | Detects if someone other than the candidate attempted the test | 4 |
| **Auxiliary device** | The candidate pairs a phone for 360° proctoring | 4 |
| **Session recording** | Records the candidate's screen. Socratease quizzes only | 4 |
| **Candidate recording** | Continuously records the candidate's camera feed | 15 |

<Tip>
The card above the sections shows the total cost, for example **1 credit per attempt**. It updates as you change the proctoring options, before you save.
</Tip>

## Who Is Informed

| Setting | What It Does | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Me, after the test** | Stores evidence (photos, audio, screenshots) on the cloud so you can review the report after the test ends. | **Keep on**: you need this to review violations. |
| **The candidate, during the test** | Warns the candidate in real time when a violation is detected. | **Keep on**: it helps candidates fix harmless issues like background noise. |

<Warning>
Turning off **Me, after the test** also turns off **Photos at random**, **Photo before test start** and **Customize the photo prompt**. They stay locked until you turn **Me, after the test** back on, because these photos are stored on the cloud for you to review.
</Warning>
