Give a colleague access to a test's results in their own AutoProctor account, or send them a file.

| Method | Works for | What the other person gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Add a collaborator** | All test types | The test on their own **Tests** page, with its results, reports and settings |
| **Share the Google Form** | Google Forms tests | The test on their own **Tests** page, with its results, reports and settings |
| [**Export the results**](/export-results-to-excel) | All test types | A CSV file of the results |
| [**Write results to Google Sheets**](/write-results-to-google-sheets) | All test types | A Google Sheet that you share with them. Needs a **Premium** or **Elite** plan |

<Warning>
The **Share** link under a test on the [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page copies the test link that candidates use to take the test. It does not give anyone access to the results.
</Warning>

## Add a Collaborator

A **collaborator** sees the test on their own **Tests** page and can open its results and proctoring reports.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Test's Settings">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Settings** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Collaborate Link">
    Click **Manage** on the collaborators card, then copy the **Collaborate link** at the bottom of the dialog.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/share-exam-results/collaborate-link.png" alt="The Collaborate link at the bottom of the Collaborators dialog" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Link">
    When the person opens the link while signed in to AutoProctor, they are added to the test and land on its results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Managing collaborators needs a **Premium** or **Elite** plan, and the person must be a member of your [team](/what-is-a-team). Collaborators get full access to the test, including its settings. See [Adding Collaborators](/add-collaborators) for the details.
</Note>

## For Google Forms

Anyone with edit access to the Google Form can reach the same AutoProctor test and its results.

<Frame>
  <video controls className="w-full rounded-xl" src="/videos/settings/timer-proctor-on-gf.mp4"></video>
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share the Google Form">
    Share the Google Form with **Editor** permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Timer + Proctor add-on">
    Ask the other users to open the **Timer + Proctor** add-on from the shared form. The test then appears on their own [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, where they click **Results**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Sharing the form alone is not enough. Each person must open the add-on at least once to be linked to the test.
</Note>
