---
title: "Missing Images and Recordings in Report"
description: "Understand why images or audio recordings may not appear in a candidate's test report despite violations being listed, and how to fix it."
---

If a candidate's report lists violations but not the images or recordings behind them, there are two possible causes.

## Scenario 1: No Images or Recordings at All

If no evidence appears at all, the **Me, after the test** switch was probably off in the test's **Proctoring** settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Proctoring section">
    On your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, then choose **Proctoring**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who is informed">
    Under **Who is informed**, check that **Me, after the test** is on. This switch stores evidence so you can review it in the report. See [Proctoring Settings](/proctoring-settings) for each option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on for future attempts">
    If the switch was off, turn it on and click **Save settings**. This does not add evidence to attempts already taken.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/proctoring-settings/who-is-informed.png" alt="The Me, after the test switch in the Proctoring section" />
</Frame>

## Scenario 2: Some Violations Have Evidence, Others Do Not

This is expected. AutoProctor stores about 20 images and audio files per attempt, so when there are more violations than that, some have no evidence file.

<Warning>
**All violations are tracked and affect the Trust Score**, even when no evidence file is stored for them.
</Warning>
