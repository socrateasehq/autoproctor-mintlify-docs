AutoProctor automatically generates an invoice and receipt for every successful payment.

## Downloading Invoices and Receipts

If you cannot find the invoice email or need to download a copy:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Click [**Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) in the left sidebar. The **Subscription & Billing** page opens on the **Overview** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the receipt">
    In the **Payments** table at the bottom of the **Overview** tab, click **View receipt** next to the payment. You can download the invoice and the receipt as PDF files from the tab that opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/invoices-and-receipts/payments.png" alt="The Payments table on the Billing page, with a View receipt link for each payment" />
</Frame>

The **Payments** table lists your **latest 24 payments**:

| You need | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| The invoice or receipt of a recent payment | **View receipt** in the **Payments** table |
| An older invoice | **See all invoices in the billing portal**, the link above the **Payments** table while you have a subscription |

See [Editing Billing Information](/edit-billing-information) for more on the billing portal.

## When Are Invoices Generated?

Invoices are generated for two types of payments:

| Payment Type | When It Happens |
|---|---|
| **Subscription creation and renewal** | When you first subscribe or when your plan auto-renews |
| **Credit top-ups** | When you purchase additional credits through Test Packs |

For each payment, an invoice and receipt are automatically emailed to the account holder.

<Note>
If you are part of a [Team](/what-is-a-team), the person who made the initial purchase receives the invoice emails. All Team Admins can also download invoices from the **Billing** page. Regular team members do not see **Billing** in the sidebar.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I did not receive an invoice email. What should I do?">
    Check your spam or junk folder. If you are part of a Team, the invoice may have been sent to the Team Admin who made the original purchase. You can also open the receipt directly from the **Payments** table on the **Billing** page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change the billing details on an invoice?">
    You can update your billing information (company name, address, GST, etc.) in the billing portal, which you open with **Manage subscription** on the **Billing** page. Changes only apply to **future invoices**. For amendments to an already-issued invoice, email **hello@autoproctor.co** with the details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are invoices available for expired subscriptions?">
    Yes. After your plan ends, the **Payments** table on the **Billing** page still lists your latest 24 payments with their receipts. For older invoices, contact support at **hello@autoproctor.co**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a consolidated invoice for multiple payments?">
    AutoProctor generates individual invoices per payment. If you need a consolidated statement, contact support at **hello@autoproctor.co** with your requirements.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
