title: "Does AutoProctor Record Video?"
description: "Learn how AutoProctor monitors candidates in real time without recording full video sessions, and why this approach is more effective."

**By default, AutoProctor does not record video.** It monitors the candidate's camera on their own device and captures evidence, such as photos, screenshots and audio clips, only when it detects a violation.

If you need full video, turn on **Candidate recording**, an [enhanced proctoring](/enhanced-proctoring) setting that continuously records the candidate's camera. See [What If I Still Want to Record Video?](#what-if-i-still-want-to-record-video) below.

## How AutoProctor's AI Monitoring Works

Traditional proctoring platforms upload a video of the candidate to their servers, and you are often expected to watch it to verify flagged incidents.

AutoProctor's AI monitors the video feed **directly on the candidate's device** and flags violations as they happen. With the default settings, no video is uploaded or stored on any server.

Think of it like a smoke detector: it runs continuously in the background, monitoring all the time, and only alerts you when something is detected.

## Key Advantages

| Advantage | How It Works |
|---|---|
| **No video review needed** | Violations are flagged automatically with supporting evidence (photos and screenshots), so you do not need to watch hours of footage |
| **Works offline** | Violations are detected even if the candidate disconnects, and the evidence syncs when they reconnect |
| **Prevents internet-off cheating** | With other platforms, candidates can turn off their internet to avoid being recorded. With AutoProctor, monitoring continues on-device regardless of connectivity |
| **Lower bandwidth requirements** | No large video files are uploaded during the test, reducing network strain for candidates with slow connections |

## What Evidence Do You Receive Instead of Video?

AutoProctor captures the evidence below. The last column is the switch that turns each one on, in the **Proctoring** section of a test's **Settings**.

| Evidence | What it captures | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| **Photos** | Snapshots from the camera feed, for example when no face or multiple faces are detected | **Camera** |
| **Screenshots** | Captures of the candidate's screen, for example when they switch tabs or applications | **Tab switching** |
| **Audio clips** | Recordings of background noise when the microphone detects suspicious audio | **Microphone** |
| **Random photos** | Periodic snapshots taken at random intervals throughout the exam | **Photos at random** |
| **Session recording** | Records of mouse clicks and keyboard activity. Socratease quizzes only | **Session recording** |

<Warning>
Evidence may occasionally be missing from a report if the candidate's device ran out of storage or if the browser restricted access to the camera or microphone mid-test. See [Missing Images and Recordings](/missing-images-and-recordings) for troubleshooting steps.
</Warning>

## What If I Still Want to Record Video?

For compliance or audit purposes, you can turn on **Candidate recording**. It continuously records the candidate's camera feed for the whole test and costs **15 additional credits per attempt**. See the [pricing page](https://www.autoproctor.co/pricing) for details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Proctoring Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Proctoring**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Candidate Recording and Save">
    With the **Proctoring** switch on, turn on **Candidate recording** under **Enhanced proctoring**. Then click **Save settings**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/does-autoproctor-record-video/recording-options.png" alt="Candidate recording switched on under Enhanced proctoring" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You then get **a full video and audio recording of the session** alongside the usual evidence. AutoProctor has two recording options, and they record different things:

| Option | What it records | Where you watch it | Extra credits per attempt | Available on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Candidate recording** | The candidate's camera feed, continuously | **Candidate recording** tab of the report | 15 | All tests |
| **Session recording** | The candidate's screen activity, including mouse clicks and keyboard input | **Session recording** tab of the report | 4 | Socratease quizzes only |

See [Enhanced Proctoring](/enhanced-proctoring) for both options and their credit costs.
