You update your payment method, company details and billing address in the **billing portal**, which you open from the **Billing** page in AutoProctor.

<Note>
If you are part of a [Team](/what-is-a-team), only Team Admins can access and edit billing information. Regular team members do not see **Billing** in the sidebar.
</Note>

## How to Update Billing Information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Click [**Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage subscription">
    Click **Manage subscription** at the top right. The billing portal opens in a new tab.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/edit-billing-information/manage-subscription.png" alt="The Subscription &amp; Billing page header with the Manage subscription button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    In the billing portal, update any of the fields listed below and save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What the Billing page offers depends on the state of your plan:

| Plan state | What the Billing page offers |
|---|---|
| Active | **Manage subscription**, which opens the billing portal |
| Last payment failed | **Clear payment**, which opens the unpaid invoice so you can pay it |
| Free trial | **Subscribe**. There are no billing details to edit yet |
| Plan ended | **Subscribe again** |

## Editable Fields

<Frame>
  <iframe className="w-full rounded-xl" style={{aspectRatio: "16/9"}} src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/d4vqixMkaJU?si=KKoUzTMD4GwszoRm&rel=0" title="How to edit billing information" frameBorder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen></iframe>
</Frame>

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Legal entity name** | The company or organization name that appears on invoices |
| **Credit card details** | Update your payment card for future charges |
| **Billing email** | The email address that receives invoices and receipts |
| **Billing address** | The address that appears on invoices |
| **GST number** | Your GST identification number (applicable to Indian customers only) |

<Warning>
Any changes you make only affect **future invoices**. If you need an amendment on an already-issued invoice, send an email to **hello@autoproctor.co** with the invoice number and the corrections needed.
</Warning>

## Keeping Your Payment Method Current

If a renewal charge still fails after several attempts, your subscription is cancelled and your credit balance resets to 0. While a payment is pending, click **Clear payment** on the **Billing** page to pay the invoice and keep your plan active.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/edit-billing-information/plan-tiles.png" alt="The Overview tiles on the Billing page, with the next charge date under Renews on" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Update your payment method before the date in the **Renews on** tile to avoid any interruption in service.
</Note>
