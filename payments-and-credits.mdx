Every test attempt by a candidate uses credits. You get credits from a subscription and from optional top-ups.

Your plan, your credit balance and your payments are on the [**Subscription & Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) page.

## Free Credits

A new Test Admin account starts on a trial with **10 free credits** and every Premium feature. Once you use them up, you need a subscription to continue.

  <img src="/images/payments-and-credits/trial-overview.png" alt="The Overview tab of Subscription & Billing during the free trial" />
</Frame>

## How Credits Are Used

Every proctored attempt uses **1 credit**. Enhanced proctoring features add extra credits to each attempt.

| What the attempt uses | Credits per attempt |
|---|---|
| **Proctored attempt** | 1 |
| **Timed-only attempt** | 1 during the trial, free with a subscription |
| **Session recording** | +4 |
| **Auxiliary device** | +4 |
| **ID card verification** | +4 |
| **Impersonation detection** | +4 |
| **Test-taker recording** (**Candidate recording** in Test Settings) | +15 |

If you turn on the four +4 features together, they add **12 credits**, not 16. See [Enhanced Proctoring](/enhanced-proctoring) for what each feature does.

## Purchase a Subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Subscribe">
    Click **Subscribe** below the credits card at the bottom of the sidebar. It opens the Pricing page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/payments-and-credits/sidebar-credits-trial.png" alt="The credits card and Subscribe button at the bottom of the sidebar" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan and Pay">
    On the [Pricing Page](https://www.autoproctor.co/pricing/), choose a plan and a billing frequency, then pay. Any trial credits you still have carry over to your new plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

AutoProctor offers **3 plans** and **3 billing frequencies**:

| Plan | Features |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Core proctoring and timer features |
| **Premium** | Standard features + Google Sheets integration, teams, and more |
| **Elite** | Premium features + API access, question banks, and advanced question types |

| Billing Frequency | Renewal Cycle |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | Renews every month on the same date |
| **Quarterly** | Renews every 3 months |
| **Yearly** | Renews every 12 months |

<Warning>
Subscriptions auto-renew. To stop the auto-deduction, you must [cancel your subscription](/cancel-subscription) before the renewal date.
</Warning>

Each plan comes with a set number of credits, for example 150. The same number is added every time the subscription renews. The **Renews on** tile on the **Overview** tab shows the date of your next charge.

## Top Up Your Subscription

If you need more credits than your plan provides, buy **credit packs**. You can buy from 1 to 1,000 packs in one purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Top up Tab">
    Go to the [**Top up**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/?t=t) tab of the **Subscription & Billing** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Number of Packs">
    Enter the **Number of packs**. The **Order summary** shows the credits you get and the total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Continue to payment** and pay on Stripe's checkout page. The credits are added to your balance a moment after you return.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/payments-and-credits/top-up.png" alt="The Top up tab with the number of packs and the order summary" />
</Frame>

Top-ups are a **one-time cost**, and they need an active plan. The credits stay available as long as your subscription is active.

<Note>
If you purchased credit packs but cannot see them in your account, see [Purchased Packs Not Showing Up](/purchased-packs-not-showing).
</Note>

## Credit Rollover

<Note>
As long as you maintain an active subscription, **credits do not expire. They roll over** into the next billing period, on top of your renewal credits.
</Note>

## Discounts

Quarterly and Yearly plans cost less than Monthly. Large top-ups also get a **volume discount**, applied automatically in the order summary.

| Packs in One Purchase | Top-Up Credits | Discount |
|---|---|---|
| 10 to 19 | 1,500 to 2,850 | 5% off |
| 20 or more | 3,000 or more | 10% off |

See the [Pricing Page](https://www.autoproctor.co/pricing/) for prices and the Pricing Calculator.
