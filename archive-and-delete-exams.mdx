<Tip>
**TL;DR:** **Close** a test to stop submissions for everyone, or **Hide** it to remove it from your own dashboard only. Both are switches in the test's **Settings**, and both can be undone from the [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page.
</Tip>

## Close or Hide a Test

Over time, your dashboard collects demo tests, completed exams, and outdated assessments. **Closing** and **hiding** both move a test out of your Active view, but they do different things.

| | Close | Hide |
|---|---|---|
| Setting name | **Close test for submissions** | **Hide from my dashboard** |
| Who is affected | You, your collaborators, and candidates | Only you |
| Can candidates still take the test? | No | Yes |
| Where the test appears afterwards | **Closed** view | **Hidden** view |
| How to undo | **Make open** | **Unhide** |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Test Settings">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Settings** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On the Switch">
    In the **General** section, turn on **Close test for submissions** or **Hide from my dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save settings**. The test leaves your Active view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find Closed and Hidden Tests

The [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page has four views at the top: **All**, **Active**, **Closed**, and **Hidden**. Active is selected by default.

| View | What it shows | Direct link |
|---|---|---|
| **All** | Every test you created or collaborate on, including closed and hidden ones | [Open](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/?t=a) |
| **Active** | Tests that are open and not hidden | [Open](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) |
| **Closed** | Tests that no one can attempt any more | [Open](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/?t=c) |
| **Hidden** | Tests you removed from your own dashboard | [Open](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/?t=h) |

## Reopen or Unhide a Test

You can bring a closed or hidden test back at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Right View">
    On the [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click [**Closed**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/?t=c) or [**Hidden**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/?t=h).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore the Test">
    Click **Make open** on a closed test, or **Unhide** on a hidden test, and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Make open** reopens the test for you and for all your collaborators. Candidates with the test link can start submitting again.
</Warning>

## Delete a Test

You can permanently delete a test from the **Closed** view. A test must be closed before it can be deleted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Close the Test">
    If the test is still open, close it first using the steps above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Closed View">
    On the [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click [**Closed**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/?t=c).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the Test">
    Click **Delete** next to the test and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Deleting a test removes it from your account **and from the accounts of all your collaborators**. A deleted test cannot be recovered. If you only want the test off your own dashboard, hide it instead.
</Warning>

## Important Notes

| Scenario | What Happens |
|---|---|
| You close a test that candidates are about to take | The test link stops working for everyone until you click **Make open** |
| You hide a test that has collaborators | Only your dashboard changes. Collaborators still see the test, and candidates can still take it |
| You delete a test that has collaborators | The test is removed for you and for every collaborator |
| You delete a Google Forms test and want to use the same form again | You can create a new test from the same Google Form |
| You want to recover a deleted test | Deleted tests cannot be recovered. Close or hide the test instead if you may need it later |
