Cancelling your AutoProctor subscription takes a few clicks.

## Cancel Your Subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription & Billing Page">
    Click **Billing** in the sidebar to open the [**Subscription & Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage subscription">
    Click **Manage subscription** at the top right. The billing portal opens in a new tab.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancel-subscription/manage-subscription.png" alt="The Subscription &amp; Billing page header with the Manage subscription button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the Plan">
    In the billing portal, click **Cancel plan**.

    <Frame>
      <img className="border rounded-xl" src="/images/pricing/cancel-plan-button.png" alt="Cancel plan button on the subscription management page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you are part of a [Team](/what-is-a-team), only a Team Admin can cancel the subscription. Regular team members do not see **Billing** in the sidebar.
</Note>

## What Happens After Cancellation

| Timing | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Immediately after cancelling** | Your subscription is **scheduled to cancel** at the end of your current billing period. The **Overview** tab shows a notice that starts with **Your plan ends on**, followed by the date. |
| **During the remaining billing period** | You and your candidates can continue using AutoProctor. You can still purchase additional credits. |
| **After the billing period ends** | The badge in the **Plan** tile changes to **Canceled**. Candidates cannot start new tests. You lose access to full results. Your credit balance resets to 0. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are my tests and data retained after cancellation?">
    Yes. The tests you created and their settings are **not deleted**. However, you cannot access the full results of those tests until you resubscribe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I resubscribe after cancellation?">
    Yes. Click **Subscribe again** on the **Overview** tab of the [**Subscription & Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) page, or below the credits card in the sidebar, and choose a plan. You can use your account again right away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I undo a scheduled cancellation?">
    Yes, until the subscription expires. Click **Manage subscription** on the **Subscription & Billing** page and reverse the scheduled cancellation in the billing portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
Download all results and proctoring reports **before** your subscription expires. After expiration, you lose access to full test results.
</Warning>
