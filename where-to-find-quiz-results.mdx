---
title: "Where Can I See My Quiz Results?"
description: "Find quiz scores and answers for your AutoProctor tests: on the Results page for Socratease Quizzes, or in the quiz platform for Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, and IFrame tests."
---

AutoProctor provides two distinct types of results for every test: **quiz results** (how candidates performed) and **proctoring results** (whether candidates maintained integrity). Where the quiz results live depends on the quiz platform you used.

## Quiz Results

<Warning>
Unless you use Socratease Quizzes, you cannot view quiz results directly on AutoProctor. Quiz results appear in the platform where you created the quiz.
</Warning>

| Quiz Platform | Where to Find Quiz Results |
|---|---|
| **Google Forms** | The **Responses** tab of your Google Form |
| **Microsoft Forms** | The **Responses** section of your Microsoft Form |
| **IFrame Tests** | The platform you embedded within the IFrame |
| **Socratease Quizzes** | The **Quiz score** column on the test's **Results** page in AutoProctor |

<Note>
If you use Google Forms and cannot see responses, refer to the troubleshooting guide on [missing Google Forms responses](/google-forms-response-not-visible).
</Note>

## Quiz Scores for Socratease Quizzes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Results Page">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Results** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Candidate">
    The **Quiz score** column shows each candidate's percentage and points, for example **88% (7/8)**. Search by name or email to find one candidate.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/where-to-find-quiz-results/quiz-score-column.png" alt="Submissions table with the Trust score and Quiz score columns" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Answers">
    Click **View answers** under the score to see every response and the points scored. If the attempt still needs grading, the link reads **Grade quiz** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For grading and the analytics views, see [Access Answers and Candidate Responses](/access-candidate-responses).

## Results Across All Your Tests

The [**Latest attempts**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/latest-attempts/) page lists every submitted attempt across all your tests, newest first. Its **Quiz score** column depends on the quiz platform of each test:

| Quiz Platform | What the Quiz score column shows |
|---|---|
| **Socratease Quizzes** | The percentage scored, with a **Graded** or **Not fully graded** badge. Click the percentage to open the candidate's answers |
| **Google Forms**, **Microsoft Forms**, **IFrame Tests** | A dash with a help icon. The scores are in the quiz platform, not in AutoProctor |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/where-to-find-quiz-results/latest-attempts-quiz-score.png" alt="Latest attempts table with a quiz score on a Socratease attempt and dashes on Google Forms attempts" />
</Frame>

## Proctoring Results

Proctoring results are a separate report for each attempt, with a [Trust Score](/what-is-trust-score). See [Where Can I See Proctoring Results?](/where-to-find-proctoring-results) to open them.

<Note>
Proctoring results are only available when you enable proctoring in your [test settings](/proctoring-settings). Tests configured with only a timer do not generate proctoring reports.
</Note>
