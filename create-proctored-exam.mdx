## Create Your First Test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to AutoProctor">
    Sign in at [AutoProctor](https://www.autoproctor.co/account/login/role/test-admin). You land on the [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-proctored-exam/tests-page.png" alt="The Tests page with the Create Test button at the top right" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Test">
    Click **Create Test** at the top right. Pick a quiz provider, paste your quiz link and give the test a title. For [Socratease](/create-socratease-quiz), you build the quiz on the next screen instead.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-proctored-exam/quiz-providers.png" alt="The four quiz providers on the Create Test page" />
    </Frame>

    See [Quiz Providers](/supported-quiz-providers) for which link to paste for each provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the test settings">
    The test's settings page opens. Open [**Timer**](/timer-settings) and [**Proctoring**](/proctoring-settings) to adjust them, then click **Save settings**. The defaults work for a first test.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-proctored-exam/test-settings-page.png" alt="The settings page of a test, with the test link at the top and the sections on the left" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
    **General** holds the [maximum attempts per candidate](/maximum-exam-attempts). Login methods, restrictions and candidate instructions are under [Advanced Settings](/advanced-exam-settings).
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the test link">
    Click **Preview** next to the test link to check it the way a candidate sees it, then click **Copy link**.

    Share the link however you reach your candidates, or use [Invite via Email](/invite-candidates-via-email). See [Best Practices](/best-practices-for-exam-creators) for tips on distributing links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View results">
    On the [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Results** next to the test. Each attempt has a **Trust score** and a report with its proctoring evidence.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-proctored-exam/test-results.png" alt="Test results page with summary tiles and the Submissions table" />
    </Frame>

    See [Where Can I See My Quiz Results?](/where-to-find-quiz-results) for a detailed guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>
