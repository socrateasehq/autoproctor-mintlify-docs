---
title: "Purchased Packs Not Showing Up"
description: "Troubleshoot why your purchased credit packs or subscription credits are not appearing in your AutoProctor account, with step-by-step solutions."
---

If your balance has not updated a few minutes after a purchase, work through these reasons, most likely first.

## 1. You Are Logged In to a Different Account

This is **the most common reason**. You paid with one email address but are signed in with another.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your current email">
    Check the **Email** field on your [**Profile**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/profile/) page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/purchased-packs-not-showing/profile-email.png" alt="The Email field on the Profile page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in with the correct account">
    AutoProctor emails a receipt for every purchase. If it went to a different address, log out and sign in with that account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you are part of a [Team](/what-is-a-team), the Team Admin receives the confirmation emails, even if you made the payment from your own account.
</Note>

## 2. Your Subscription Has Expired

If a renewal payment fails, your subscription is cancelled and **your credit balance resets to 0**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your subscription status">
    On the **Overview** tab of the [**Subscription & Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) page, check the badge in the **Plan** tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your payment method">
    Make sure the card on file is valid. You can update it via [Editing Billing Information](/edit-billing-information).

    If the badge reads **Past due**, click **Clear payment** at the top right to pay the pending invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resubscribe if needed">
    If your plan has ended, click **Subscribe again** and choose a plan on the [Pricing Page](https://www.autoproctor.co/pricing/).
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Badge | What It Means | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Your subscription is running and you can top up | Check the other reasons on this page |
| **Past due** | Your last payment did not go through. Top up is paused until it is cleared | Click **Clear payment** |
| **Canceled** or **Inactive** | Your plan has ended and its credits were reset | Click **Subscribe again** |
| **Trial** | You are on the free trial and have not subscribed with this account | Click **Subscribe** |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/purchased-packs-not-showing/plan-ended.png" alt="The Overview tab after a plan has ended" />
</Frame>

## 3. Your Team Association Changed

Team members share one subscription and credit balance. If you left a [Team](/what-is-a-team) or were removed, you no longer see its credits. Check your status on the [**Team**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/team/) page.

<Warning>
Leaving a team means you lose access to the team's shared credits. Make sure this is intentional before you leave.
</Warning>

## 4. Error at AutoProctor's End

If none of the above apply, the issue may be on our side.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take a screenshot">
    Take a screenshot of the **Overview** tab of the [**Subscription & Billing**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/billing/) page, showing **Credits remaining** and the **Payments** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Send the screenshot and your purchase confirmation email to **hello@autoproctor.co**. Our team will investigate and resolve the issue.
  </Step>
</Steps>
