An unsubmitted test occurs when a candidate starts a test but does not click the green **Submit** button at the top of the test window. The candidate may have viewed questions and entered answers, but their test was never formally submitted.

</Frame>

## Viewing Unsubmitted Tests on the Results Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Results Page">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Results** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Unsubmitted Tile">
    The tiles at the top of the page count the attempts that were **Started**, **Submitted** and **Unsubmitted**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/view-unsubmitted-exams/unsubmitted-tile.png" alt="The Unsubmitted tile with its Show in table link" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Show in Table">
    Click **Show in table** on the **Unsubmitted** tile. The **Submissions** table below then lists only the unsubmitted attempts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Show in table** sets the **Submitted** filter above the table to **No**. To go back to the full list, click the **×** on the **Submitted** filter.

## How an Unsubmitted Attempt Appears in the Table

An unsubmitted attempt has a start time and nothing else. Its other columns show a dash until the candidate submits. On a proctored test, it still has a **View report** button.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/view-unsubmitted-exams/unsubmitted-rows.png" alt="Submissions table filtered to unsubmitted attempts" />
</Frame>

To remove an attempt, click the delete icon at the end of its row and confirm with **Yes, delete**. This permanently deletes the attempt and its evidence.

<Note>
The [**Latest attempts**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/latest-attempts/) page lists submitted attempts only. Unsubmitted attempts appear only on the **Results** page of their own test.
</Note>

## Proctoring Report for an Unsubmitted Test

The proctoring report is created after an attempt is submitted. Click **View report** on an unsubmitted attempt, and its **Proctoring** tab says the test has not been marked as finished yet.

{/* screenshot: attempt report of an unsubmitted attempt on a proctored Google Forms test, showing the "Submitted" card reading "Not submitted", the "Proctoring" tab, and the message "The test has not been marked as finished yet" and the "Mark as Finished" button */}

An attempt counts as submitted once the candidate submits it, or once the timer submits it automatically. See [Timer Settings](/timer-settings) for automatic submission.

To generate the proctoring report, click **Mark as Finished**. The report loads a few seconds later.

<Warning>
If you mark a test as finished and the candidate is still attempting the test, newer violations will be ignored.
</Warning>

## Quiz Answers for an Unsubmitted Test

For Socratease quizzes, an unsubmitted attempt has no answers to view. Its report's **Results** tab reads "No answers recorded for this attempt yet."
