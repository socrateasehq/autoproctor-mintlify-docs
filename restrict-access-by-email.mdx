---
title: "Restrict Test Access by Email Address"
description: "Limit who can take your test by restricting access to specific email domains or individual email addresses using Login restrictions."
---

**Login restrictions** let you control exactly who can take your test by filtering candidates based on their email address. This prevents unauthorized access even if someone shares or discovers your test link.

## How to Set Up Email Restrictions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Access & login Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Access & login** from the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Domain or Email Restrictions">
    In **Login restrictions**, enter the email domains or specific email addresses you want to allow, separated by commas. You can combine both types in the same field (see the examples below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| To allow | Enter in **Login restrictions** |
|---|---|
| Everyone at one domain | `@abc.com` |
| Everyone at several domains | `@abc.com, @xyz.com` |
| One domain plus specific people | `@abc.com, guest@gmail.com` |
| Only specific people | `ana@abc.com, guest@gmail.com` |

<Warning>
Separate entries with commas, not spaces. An entry that contains a space is rejected when you save.
</Warning>

## Domain-Based Restrictions

You can restrict access to candidates whose email addresses end with specific domains. For example, entering `@abc.com` and `@xyz.com` allows only candidates with those email domains to take the test.

## Specific Email Restrictions

You can also permit individual email addresses alongside domain restrictions. This is useful when most candidates share a domain but a few external participants need access.

For example, you can allow all users whose email ends with `@abc.com` plus specific individual email addresses like `guest@gmail.com`.

{/* screenshot: Access & login section of Test Settings for a Google Forms test, cropped to the "Login restrictions" field containing "@abc.com, guest@gmail.com" */}

<Note>
Email restrictions work in conjunction with the candidate's [login method](/candidate-login-methods). The candidate must sign in with an email address that matches one of the allowed domains or addresses you specified.
</Note>
