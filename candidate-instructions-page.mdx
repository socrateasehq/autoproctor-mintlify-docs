Every AutoProctor test begins with an instructions page that explains how proctoring works, which features are active, and what candidates should expect. The page content adapts automatically based on your test configuration, and candidates can also take a demo test from this page to verify device compatibility.

## How the Instructions Page Works

The instructions page is **automatically active on all tests**. Candidates see it immediately after clicking the test link. You cannot disable this page, because it ensures every candidate understands the test rules before they begin.

A test's **Settings** call this page the **start page**. You control what you add to it from the **Before the test** section.

| On the page | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| An explanation of which proctoring features are enabled for the test | Automatic, based on the test's proctoring settings |
| Browser and device requirements | Automatic |
| A link to a demo test so candidates can verify their setup | Automatic |
| Any custom instructions you add | **Show additional instructions** (Elite plan required) |
| Extra fields the candidate must fill in | **Collect candidate details** (Premium plan required) |

## Adding Custom Instructions

You can include your own instructions (maximum 200 words) that appear on the instructions page alongside the default content.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Before the Test Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Before the test** from the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Additional Instructions">
    Under **Additional instructions**, turn on **Show additional instructions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Custom Instructions">
    Type your text in the **Instructions** box. A counter below the box shows how many of the 200 words you have used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Gathering Additional Information from Candidates

Use **Collect candidate details** to ask every candidate for extra information, such as a phone number or a student ID. The fields appear under **Your Details** on the instructions page, and candidates must complete all of them before starting the test.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Before the Test Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Before the test** from the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Candidate Details">
    Under **Candidate details**, turn on **Collect candidate details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Fields">
    Click one of the **Suggestions** to add it, or click **Add field**, type a field name and press Enter. To remove a field, click the **x** on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the Fields in Order (Optional)">
    Drag a field to move it, or select it, hold Alt and press an arrow key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fields per test | Up to 10 |
| Length of a field name | Up to 250 characters and 50 words |
| Required or optional | Every field is required |
| Suggested fields | **Phone Number**, **Student ID**, **College Name**, **Graduation Year**, **Work Experience (in years)** |
| Field order | The order candidates see, and the column order in the CSV export |

The answers appear on the test's results and in the export.

<Note>
**Collect candidate details** requires a **Premium** or **Elite** plan.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I preview the instructions page before sharing the test link?">
    Yes. At the top of the test's **Settings** page, click **Preview** next to **Copy link**. The page opens in a new tab, exactly as candidates will see it. You can also open your own test link. Viewing the instructions page does not consume a credit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does viewing the instructions page use a credit?">
    No. Credits are only deducted when a candidate successfully starts the test, not when they view the instructions page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I remove sections from the instructions page?">
    No. The default sections are fixed and cannot be removed. You can only add additional instructions on top of the existing content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
