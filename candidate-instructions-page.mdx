Every AutoProctor test begins with an instructions page that explains how proctoring works and which features are active. Candidates can also take a demo test from this page to check their device.

  <img className="border rounded-xl" src="/images/getting-started/instructions-page.png" alt="AutoProctor instructions page shown to candidates before a test begins" />
</Frame>

## How the Instructions Page Works

Candidates see the instructions page right after clicking the test link. It is **active on all tests** and cannot be disabled.

A test's **Settings** call this page the **start page**. You control what you add to it from the **Before the test** section.

| On the page | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| An explanation of which proctoring features are enabled for the test | Automatic, based on the test's proctoring settings |
| Browser and device requirements | Automatic |
| A link to a demo test so candidates can verify their setup | Automatic |
| Any custom instructions you add | **Show additional instructions** (Elite plan required) |
| Extra fields the candidate must fill in | **Collect candidate details** (Premium plan required) |

## Adding Custom Instructions

You can add your own instructions (maximum 200 words). They appear on the instructions page alongside the default content.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Before the Test Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Before the test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Additional Instructions">
    Turn on **Show additional instructions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Custom Instructions">
    Type your text in the **Instructions** box and click **Save settings**. The text is plain text and line breaks are kept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/candidate-instructions-page/additional-instructions.png" alt="Show additional instructions switched on, with text in the Instructions box" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Adding custom instructions requires an **Elite plan** subscription. See [Elite Features](/elite-plan-features) for what the plan includes.
</Note>

## Gathering Additional Information from Candidates

Use **Collect candidate details** to ask every candidate for extra information, such as a phone number or a student ID. Candidates must complete all the fields before starting the test.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Before the Test Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Before the test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Candidate Details">
    Turn on **Collect candidate details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Fields">
    Click a suggestion, or click **Add field** and type a field name. Drag the fields to reorder them, then click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/candidate-instructions-page/candidate-details.png" alt="Collect candidate details switched on, with two fields and the suggestions below" />
</Frame>

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fields per test | Up to 10 |
| Length of a field name | Up to 250 characters and 50 words |
| Required or optional | Every field is required |
| Field order | The order candidates see, and the column order in the CSV export |

The answers appear on the test's results and in the export.

<Note>
**Collect candidate details** requires a **Premium** or **Elite** plan.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I preview the instructions page before sharing the test link?">
    Yes. At the top of the test's **Settings** page, click **Preview** next to **Copy link**. The page opens in a new tab, exactly as candidates will see it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does viewing the instructions page use a credit?">
    No. Credits are only deducted when a candidate successfully starts the test, not when they view the instructions page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I remove sections from the instructions page?">
    No. The default sections are fixed and cannot be removed. You can only add additional instructions on top of the existing content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
