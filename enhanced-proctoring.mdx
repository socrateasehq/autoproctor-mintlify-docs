Enhanced Proctoring adds advanced identity verification and monitoring features beyond standard proctoring. These features help prevent impersonation, detect AI tools, and create a comprehensive record of each test attempt, including full candidate video.

<Note>
Most enhanced proctoring features cost **4 additional credits per attempt**. **Candidate recording** costs **15 additional credits per attempt**. If you turn on multiple enhanced features, the credit cost is cumulative.
</Note>

## How to Turn On Enhanced Proctoring

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Proctoring Section">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Settings** next to the test, and choose **Proctoring** from the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn Proctoring On">
    Turn on the switch at the top right of the **Proctoring** section. The enhanced options only show while proctoring is **On**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On the Features You Need">
    Under **Enhanced proctoring**, turn on each feature you want. The badge next to each one shows its cost, for example **+4 credits / attempt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Features

### ID Card Verification

**ID card verification** confirms the candidate's identity by matching their uploaded ID against their test photo and entered name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on ID card verification">
    Turn on **ID card verification** under **Enhanced proctoring** in the **Proctoring** section of your test's Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate uploads their ID">
    When starting the test, the candidate is prompted to upload a photo of their ID card. Accepted documents include candidate ID cards, passports, driver's licenses, and government-issued IDs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AutoProctor verifies identity">
    The system compares the photo and name on the ID card against the photo taken before the test starts and the name the candidate entered on the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="ID verification screen before test start">
  <img style={{transform: "scale(1.008)"}} src="/images/taking-tests/id-verification.png" alt="ID verification screen before test start" />
</Frame>

<Note>
**Photo before test start** must be on under **What gets tracked** for ID card verification to work effectively.
</Note>

### Impersonation Detection

**Impersonation detection** monitors whether someone other than the original candidate attempts or continues the test. AutoProctor compares periodic photos taken during the test against the initial photo to detect face changes.

### Auxiliary Device (360° Proctoring)

The **Auxiliary device** feature enables 360-degree proctoring by pairing the candidate's phone with their laptop. This provides a secondary camera angle that captures the candidate's physical environment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Auxiliary device">
    Turn on **Auxiliary device** under **Enhanced proctoring** in the **Proctoring** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate pairs their phone">
    When the test starts, the candidate scans a QR code on their laptop screen using their phone camera. This pairs the two devices.

    <Frame caption="Auxiliary device pairing screen before test start">
      <img style={{transform: "scaleX(1.019) scaleY(1.04)"}} src="/images/taking-tests/aux-device.png" alt="Auxiliary device pairing screen before test start" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phone provides secondary monitoring">
    The phone camera captures the candidate's desk, surroundings, and screen from a different angle. This also helps detect AI tools that display answers as overlays on the screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the test, the candidate's report has an **Auxiliary device** tab with the captures from the phone.

<Frame caption="Auxiliary device evidence captures">
  <img src="/images/taking-tests/aux-device-evidence.png" alt="Auxiliary device evidence captures" />
</Frame>

### Session Recording

**Session recording** captures a complete record of the candidate's screen activity throughout the test, including mouse clicks and keyboard input. This creates a reviewable timeline of the entire test attempt, which you play back in the **Session recording** tab of the candidate's report.

<Note>
**Session recording** is available on Socratease quizzes only. On other tests, its switch is disabled and reads **Can be enabled only on Socratease quizzes**.
</Note>

{/* screenshot: a candidate's attempt report for a Socratease quiz with the "Session recording" tab selected and the recording player visible */}

### Candidate Recording

**Candidate recording** continuously records the candidate's camera feed throughout the entire test. This lets the examiner watch the candidate's behavior during the test, providing the most complete evidence of test integrity. You watch it in the **Candidate recording** tab of the candidate's report.

<Note>
**Candidate recording** costs **15 additional credits per attempt** and is **not included** in the enhanced proctoring bundle discount.
</Note>

[Try the enhanced proctoring demo](https://www.autoproctor.co/tests/aux-demo/) to see these features in action before enabling them on your test.

## Credit Cost Summary

These credits are added to the **1 base credit** of every proctored attempt.

| Feature | Extra Credits per Attempt | Available On |
|---|---|---|
| **ID card verification** | 4 | All tests |
| **Impersonation detection** | 4 | All tests |
| **Auxiliary device** (360°) | 4 | All tests |
| **Session recording** | 4 | Socratease quizzes only |
| **Candidate recording** | 15 | All tests |
| All 4 features combined (excl. Candidate recording) | 12 (25% bundle discount) | Socratease quizzes only |

<Note>
The 25% bundle discount applies when you turn on **ID card verification**, **Impersonation detection**, **Auxiliary device**, and **Session recording** together. Socratease tests show this as **Special bundle pricing** at the top of the **Enhanced proctoring** group. **Candidate recording** is billed separately at 15 credits per attempt.
</Note>

The card above the sections adds everything up for you. With only **ID card verification** on, it reads **5 credits per attempt**, with the breakdown **1 base + ID card verification (+4)**.
