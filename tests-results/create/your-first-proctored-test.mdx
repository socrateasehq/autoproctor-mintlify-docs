<Tip>
Create a proctored test, share the link with candidates, and view results — all in under 5 minutes.
</Tip>

## Create Your First Test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to AutoProctor">
    Go to [autoproctor.co](https://www.autoproctor.co/) and sign in with your **Google** or **Microsoft** account. You'll land on your dashboard, where all your tests are listed.

    <Frame caption="AutoProctor dashboard for a new user">
      <img style={{maxWidth: "56cqi"}} src="/images/getting-started/new-user-dashboard.png" alt="AutoProctor dashboard for a new user" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new test">
    Click **Create Test** on your dashboard. Choose a quiz provider:

    | Provider | When to Use |
    |---|---|
    | **Google Forms** | You already have a Google Form |
    | **Microsoft Forms** | You already have a Microsoft Form |
    | **Socratease** | You want to create a quiz directly on AutoProctor |
    | **IFrame** | You use another platform (TypeForm, ClassMarker, etc.) |

    <Frame caption="Quiz provider selection screen">
      <img className="rounded-xl border" style={{maxWidth: "56cqi"}} src="/images/creating-tests/all-test-types.png" alt="Quiz provider selection screen" />
    </Frame>

    Select your provider and follow the prompts to connect your quiz.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure test settings">
    Click **Settings** on the test to configure:

    - **Main Settings** — Enable the timer and proctoring, set [maximum attempts](/tests-results/access-limits/maximum-attempts), and name your test
    - **[Timer Settings](/tests-results/create/timer-settings)** — Set a duration, start window, and submission deadline
    - **[Proctoring Settings](/tests-results/create/proctoring-settings)** — Enable camera, microphone, tab-switching detection, and more
    - **[Advanced Settings](/tests-results/create/advanced-settings)** — Choose login methods and add restrictions

    <Frame caption="Test settings page with key sections">
      <img className="border rounded-xl" style={{maxWidth: "56cqi"}} src="/images/getting-started/configure-test-settings.png" alt="Test settings page with key sections" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
    For your first test, the default proctoring settings work well. You can fine-tune them later based on your needs.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the link yourself">
    Before sharing, click the test link yourself to verify the candidate experience. Confirm that the **instructions page**, **camera check**, and **proctoring setup** all load correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the test link">
    Copy the test link from your dashboard and share it with candidates via:

    - **Email** — or use [Invite via Email](/tests-results/access-limits/inviting-candidates-via-email) for direct invitations
    - **Your LMS** or classroom platform
    - **Any messaging tool**

    <Frame caption="Copying the test link from the dashboard">
      <img style={{maxWidth: "56cqi"}} src="/images/getting-started/copy-test-link.gif" alt="Copying the test link from the dashboard" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View results">
    Once candidates complete the test, go to your [dashboard](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) and click **Results** to see:

    - **Trust scores** for each candidate
    - **Proctoring violation** details
    - **Photos, recordings, and evidence**
    - **Quiz scores** (only if you used Socratease)

    <Frame caption="Sample results page showing submissions, trust scores, and quiz scores">
      <img
        className="rounded-xl"
        style={{maxWidth: "56cqi"}}
        src="/images/getting-started/sample-results.png"
        alt="AutoProctor results page showing submission summary and candidate scores"
      />
    </Frame>

    For a detailed guide, see [Where Can I See My Quiz Results?](/tests-results/results/how-to-see-results).
  </Step>
</Steps>
