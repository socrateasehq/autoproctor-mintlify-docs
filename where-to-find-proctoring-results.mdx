---
title: "Where Can I See Proctoring Results?"
description: "Access and review proctoring results for your tests on AutoProctor, including Trust Scores, violation details, and candidate-level reports."
---

After candidates complete a proctored test, AutoProctor compiles their monitoring data into a proctoring report for each attempt.

<Warning>
Proctoring results are only generated when you enable proctoring in your [test settings](/proctoring-settings). If you configured your test with only a timer, no proctoring data is available.
</Warning>

## Viewing Proctoring Results

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Results Page">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Results** next to the test.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/where-to-find-proctoring-results/test-row.png" alt="A test on the Tests page with the Results button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Submissions Table">
    The **Submissions** table lists one row per attempt, with the [Trust Score](/what-is-trust-score) of each attempt when proctoring is on.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/where-to-find-proctoring-results/submissions-table.png" alt="Submissions table with a trust score and View report on each row" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Report">
    Click a candidate's **Trust score**, or click **View report** at the end of the row. The report opens on the **Proctoring** tab.

    <Frame>
      <img className="border rounded-xl" src="/images/results/proctoring-summary.png" alt="Proctoring tab of a report with detected violations and evidence" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Trust score** cell tells you whether a report is ready:

| Trust score shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| A percentage | The attempt is scored. Click the percentage to open the report |
| **Pending** | The attempt is submitted and its score is still being calculated. **View report** already opens the evidence |
| A dash | The attempt is not submitted yet, or it was not proctored |

<Note>
The proctoring report appears a few minutes after the attempt is submitted. For attempts that were started but never submitted, see [Unsubmitted Tests](/view-unsubmitted-exams).
</Note>

## Finding the Attempts to Review

The controls above the **Submissions** table narrow and reorder the list.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Started** filter | Shows attempts started on, before, after or between the dates you pick |
| **Submitted** filter | Shows only submitted attempts (**Yes**) or only unsubmitted ones (**No**) |
| **Trust score** filter | Shows attempts above a percentage, below a percentage, or between two percentages |
| **Sort** | Orders the table by **Started**, **Submitted** or **Trust score** |
| **Columns** | Shows or hides columns. **Device type** and **Attempt label** are hidden until you turn them on |

## What the Report Shows

The report is split into tabs, one for each kind of evidence.

| Tab | What it shows | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Results** | The candidate's answers and points scored | Socratease Quizzes only |
| **Proctoring** | The Trust Score and the list of violations with evidence | Proctoring was on for the attempt |
| **Auxiliary device** | Captures from the candidate's phone | **Auxiliary device** was on |
| **Session recording** | A replay of the candidate's screen activity | **Session recording** was on |
| **Candidate recording** | The continuous recording of the candidate's camera | **Candidate recording** was on |

The last three tabs belong to [enhanced proctoring](/enhanced-proctoring).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/where-to-find-proctoring-results/report-header.png" alt="Top of an attempt report with its summary cards and tabs" />
</Frame>

The **Proctoring** tab for each candidate includes:

| Data Point | Description |
|---|---|
| **Trust Score** | An overall integrity rating based on detected violations |
| **Violations list** | Each violation type with timestamp and evidence |
| **Screenshots** | Random photos captured during the test session |
| **Session timeline** | When the candidate started, paused, or submitted the test |

## Proctoring Results Across All Your Tests

The [**Latest attempts**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/latest-attempts/) page lists every submitted attempt across all your tests, newest first. Click the **Trust score**, or **View report** under the test title, to open that attempt's report.
