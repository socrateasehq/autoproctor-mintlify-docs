---
title: "Resuming Test Attempts"
description: "Understand how AutoProctor handles test resumption when candidates disconnect, close the browser, or leave a test mid-attempt."
---

<Tip>
**Resuming** means any way the candidate returns to the test: clicking the test link again, reloading the page in the browser, or coming back after a while and letting the browser auto-reload.
</Tip>

When a candidate leaves a test mid-attempt, AutoProctor either resumes the previous attempt or creates a new one. Which one depends on your test type and settings.

## Resume Behavior by Test Type

| Test Type | Resume Behavior | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Socratease Quiz | Always resumes | Loads previous responses automatically; candidates must complete the existing attempt before starting a new one |
| Microsoft Forms | Configurable | Controlled by the **Auto-resume** switch. AutoProctor reopens the same attempt, but Microsoft Forms does not keep unsubmitted answers |
| Google Forms | Configurable | Controlled by the **Auto-resume** switch and Google Forms autosave setting |
| IFrame Tests | Configurable | Controlled by the **Auto-resume** switch |

## Configuring the Resume Feature

For Google Forms, Microsoft Forms and IFrame tests, the **Auto-resume** switch controls resume behavior.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open test settings">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Settings** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn Auto-resume on or off">
    In the **General** section, use the **Auto-resume** switch, then click **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/resume-exam-attempts/auto-resume-switch.png" alt="The Auto-resume switch in the General section of Test Settings" />
</Frame>

| Auto-resume | When the candidate returns |
|---|---|
| **On** | AutoProctor reopens the previous attempt. Whether answers are kept depends on the quiz platform's own autosave |
| **Off** | AutoProctor creates a new, blank attempt. The incomplete attempt still appears in your results |

<Note>
The **Auto-resume** switch does not appear for Socratease quizzes, because they always resume. For Google Forms, you must also leave **Disable auto-save for all respondents** off in Google Forms itself. See [Resuming Google Forms Tests](/resume-google-forms-exam) for detailed configuration.
</Note>

## How Timers Interact with Resumption

[Timer settings](/timer-settings) change what happens when a candidate comes back.

| Timer setting | Effect on a candidate who comes back |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | The clock keeps running while the candidate is away. Once the duration expires, the test can no longer be resumed |
| **Cannot start before** / **Cannot start after** | Checked against the original start time, not the time of return. A candidate who started in time can still resume after the deadline |
| **Hard stop** | Checked against the time of return. Once it has passed, the test does not load |

For example, a candidate starts a 60-minute test at 10:00 AM and disconnects at 10:30 AM. Resuming at 10:50 AM leaves only 10 minutes.

<Warning>
The **Hard stop** overrides all other timer settings at resumption time. If it has passed, the candidate cannot resume regardless of remaining test duration.
</Warning>
