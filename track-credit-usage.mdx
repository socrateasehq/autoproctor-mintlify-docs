---
title: "Track Your Test Pack Credit Usage"
description: "Monitor how your credits are being consumed across tests, candidates, and team members using the Usage page in your AutoProctor account."
---

The **Usage** page shows where your credits go. You can see usage per test, per candidate attempt and, if you are part of a team, per team member.

## Viewing Your Usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Usage page">
    Click [**Usage**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/usage/) in the left sidebar, then pick the **Tests** or **Credits** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Tab | One row for | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **Tests** | Each test you created that has at least one attempt | See how many candidates attempted each test |
| [**Credits**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/usage/?t=c) | Each candidate's attempt that used credits | See how many credits each attempt used and the balance after it |

<Tip>
Click **Add credits** at the top right of the Usage page to buy more credits.
</Tip>

## The Tests Tab

The **Tests** tab lists your tests with their number of attempts. Click a test's title to open its results.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/track-credit-usage/usage-tests.png" alt="The Tests tab of the Usage page" />
</Frame>

<Note>
If a test has been shared with multiple test administrators, only the **primary admin (the test creator)** sees it listed on their Usage page.
</Note>

## The Credits Tab

The **Credits** tab has one row for every attempt that used credits, newest first.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Date & Time** | When the candidate started the test |
| **Transaction Reason** | The candidate's email and the test they started, along with any enhanced proctoring features that were on |
| **Credits Used** | The credits deducted for the attempt, shown as a negative number |
| **Balance Remaining** | Your credit balance right after the attempt |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/track-credit-usage/usage-credits.png" alt="The Credits tab of the Usage page" />
</Frame>

Credits are deducted when a candidate **starts** a test. [Enhanced proctoring](/enhanced-proctoring) features add credits per attempt, so a row can show more than 1. See [Payments and Credits Explained](/payments-and-credits) for the full credit rules.

Search by **Candidate email**, filter by **Date & Time**, or click **Export** to download the rows as a CSV file.

## Team Usage

If you are part of a [Team](/what-is-a-team), the Usage page shows your own tests and attempts by default. **Team admins** can also see usage across the whole team.

### Credits Used by the Whole Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Credits tab">
    Go to the [**Usage**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/usage/?t=c) page and select **Credits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Team Credit Usage">
    Click **Team Credit Usage** above the table. The table lists the attempts of **every team member's tests**, with a **Test Admin** column showing who created each test. Click **My Credit Usage** to go back to your own attempts.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/track-credit-usage/team-credit-usage.png" alt="Team Credit Usage selected on the Credits tab" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Tests of One Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    Click [**Team**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/team/) in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click usage on a member card">
    Click **usage** next to a member's attempt count. The **Test Usage** page opens with that member's tests.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/track-credit-usage/member-usage-links.png" alt="Member cards on the Team page, each with a usage link" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Who Can See What

| View | Team Admin | Regular Member |
|---|---|---|
| Your own **Tests** and **Credits** tabs | Yes | Yes |
| **Team Credit Usage** | Yes | No |
| Attempt count on every member card | Yes | Yes |
| **usage** link on a member card | Every member | Your own card only |
