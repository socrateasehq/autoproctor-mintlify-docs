---
title: "Resuming Google Forms Tests"
description: "Configure whether candidates can resume Google Forms tests after closing them mid-attempt by managing autosave and auto-resume settings."
---

Resuming a Google Forms test depends on two settings that must match: **Disable auto-save for all respondents** in Google Forms and **Auto-resume** in AutoProctor.

## To Allow Test Resumption

Candidates pick up where they left off after closing the test.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keep autosave enabled in Google Forms">
    Make sure **Disable auto-save for all respondents** is **OFF** in your Google Forms settings. This is the default.

    <Frame>
      <img className="border rounded-xl" style={{maxWidth: "49cqi"}} src="/images/settings/gforms-autosave-enabled.png" alt="Google Forms settings with Disable auto-save turned off" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable auto-resume in AutoProctor">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Settings** next to the test. In the **General** section, turn **Auto-resume** on and click **Save settings**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/resume-exam-attempts/auto-resume-switch.png" alt="The Auto-resume switch in the General section of Test Settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## To Prevent Test Resumption

Each visit to the test link creates a new, blank attempt.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Disable autosave in Google Forms">
    Turn **Disable auto-save for all respondents** **ON** in your Google Forms settings.

    <Frame>
      <img className="border rounded-xl" style={{maxWidth: "49cqi"}} src="/images/settings/gforms-autosave-disabled.png" alt="Google Forms settings with Disable auto-save turned on" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable auto-resume in AutoProctor">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Settings** next to the test. In the **General** section, turn **Auto-resume** off and click **Save settings**.

    {/* screenshot: General section of Test Settings for a Google Forms test, cropped to the switch rows, with "Auto-resume" switched off */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings Combination Reference

| Disable auto-save (Google Forms) | Auto-resume (AutoProctor) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| OFF (default) | ON (default) | Candidates resume where they left off |
| ON | OFF | Each visit starts a fresh, blank attempt |
| OFF | OFF | New attempt loads, but Google Forms may show previously saved answers, which confuses candidates |
| ON | ON | AutoProctor tries to resume, but Google Forms has no saved draft, so the candidate sees a blank form with reduced time |

Tests resume by default unless you change either setting.

<Warning>
If the two settings are mismatched, candidates may see a partially filled form on a new attempt or lose their saved progress. Always configure both settings to match.
</Warning>
