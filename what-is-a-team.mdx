The Team feature lets multiple users share a single billing account and credit pool.

<Note>
The Team feature is available on the **Premium** and **Elite** plans, and during the free trial. On the Standard plan, the Team page shows an **Upgrade subscription** button instead.
</Note>

## What is a Team?

A Team is a group of AutoProctor users linked to the same billing account.

| Benefit | What it means |
|---|---|
| **One shared credit pool** | All members draw from the same credit balance, which team admins top up from the **Billing** page |
| **Cost savings** | Pooling credits avoids duplicate purchases and lets you use bulk discounts |
| **Test collaboration** | Add team members as [collaborators](/add-collaborators) on your tests |
| **Usage in one place** | The Team page shows every member's test attempts, and team admins see the credits used by the whole team |

**Example:** a school creates one team, so all its test creators share one subscription and one credit pool.

## Creating a Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    Click [**Team**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/team/) in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your team">
    Type a name under **Team name** and click **Create team**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/what-is-a-team/create-team.png" alt="The Team page before a team exists, with the Team name field and the Create team button" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
When you create a team, your current subscription and remaining credits become the team's. You become the **team admin**.
</Warning>

## Inviting Members

Members join a team through its **join link**. Every member of the team can see and share the link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the join link">
    On the [**Team**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/team/) page, click the link under **Join link** to copy it. Send it to the people you want on your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The invitee opens the link">
    The invitee opens the link while signed in to AutoProctor and joins the team immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A join link does not work in these cases:

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| The team does not have an active subscription | Ask the person who shared the link to check the team's plan |
| You are already part of another team | Leave your current team from the **Team** page, then open the link again |
| You have your own active subscription | Cancel it from the **Billing** page first. You can keep using it until it expires |

<Warning>
While you are in a team, you use the team's plan and its shared credits, not your own. If you leave the team later, you lose access to the team's credits and tests.
</Warning>

## The Team Page

Once you are in a team, the [**Team**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/team/) page shows four tiles at the top:

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Credits remaining** | The balance shared across all members |
| **Plan** | The team's plan and its status. Team admins also get a **Manage on Billing** link |
| **Members** | The number of people in the team, split into admins and members |
| **Test attempts** | All attempts across the team, with a **view usage** link to the Usage page |

## Team Admin Role

A **Team Admin** can do more than a regular member:

| Capability | Team Admin | Regular Member |
|---|---|---|
| Rename the team (**Update** next to **Team name**) | Yes | No |
| Promote a member with **Make admin** | Yes | No |
| Demote another admin with **Remove as admin** | Yes | No |
| Take a member off the team with **Remove** | Yes | No |
| Open the **Billing** page | Yes | No |
| View **Team Credit Usage** and any member's tests | Yes | No |
| See the team name, join link, members and attempt counts | Yes | Yes |
| Use shared credits for tests | Yes | Yes |

A team always has at least one admin. The only admin of a team cannot be demoted or removed.

<Note>
Regular team members do not see **Billing** in the sidebar. If you need billing access, ask a Team Admin to promote you.
</Note>

## Removing Members and Leaving a Team

| Action | Who can do it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Remove** on a member card | Team admins | The member loses access to the team's shared credits and tests, and is moved to their own account with no credits |
| **Leave team** | Any member | You lose access to the team's shared credits and tests. The team keeps its own balance |
| **Leave team** as the last member | The last member | The team is deleted |

<Warning>
If you are the only member of a team with an active subscription, **Leave team** is disabled. Change the plan from the **Billing** page before leaving. If your team has developer credentials and you are its last member, leaving stops those credentials from working, and this cannot be undone.
</Warning>
