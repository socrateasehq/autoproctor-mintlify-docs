**Socratease** is AutoProctor's built-in quiz tool. You build the quiz, proctor it and review results in one place.

- A **single [submit button](/how-to-submit-quiz)**, so candidates never confuse submitting the quiz with submitting the proctored test
- **14 question formats**, plus Excel imports and question banks

## How to Create a Socratease Quiz

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to AutoProctor">
    Sign in at [autoproctor.co](https://www.autoproctor.co/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Test">
    On your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Create Test**, then **Socratease Quizzes**, then **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-socratease-quiz/socratease-card.png" alt="The Socratease Quizzes card on the Create Test page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Questions">
    Add your questions using any of the [supported question types](/socratease-question-types), with points and time limits for each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Set up your [quiz settings](/socratease-quiz-settings), such as display mode, result visibility and randomization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Proceed">
    Click **Save & Proceed** in the quiz editor. AutoProctor creates the proctored test and opens its settings, where you set the [timer](/timer-settings) and [proctoring](/proctoring-settings).

    {/* screenshot: Socratease quiz editor with a quiz open, cropped to the "Save" and "Save & Proceed" buttons side by side, with the tooltip "Continue to AutoProctor Settings" showing on "Save & Proceed" */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the Test">
    Click **Copy link** at the top of the settings page and share the link with your candidates.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-socratease-quiz/settings-header.png" alt="The header of a Socratease test's settings page, with the test link and Copy link button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Results">
    On your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page, click **Results** next to the test. Quiz scores and proctoring reports are in one place.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/create-socratease-quiz/test-results.png" alt="Results page of a Socratease quiz with summary tiles and the Submissions table" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Save** keeps you in the quiz editor and does not create a proctored test. Until you click **Save & Proceed**, the quiz has no test link and does not appear on your **Tests** page.
</Warning>

## Socratease Quizzes in the Sidebar

Click **Socratease Quizzes** in the sidebar to see every quiz you have built, or to start a new one.

A quiz that is not yet a proctored test carries a **Not Added** tag. Open it and click **Save & Proceed** to create its test.

{/* screenshot: Socratease Quizzes page opened from the sidebar, cropped to the notice that explains the "Not Added" tag and the quiz cards below it, with at least one card carrying the "Not Added" tag */}

## Edit a Quiz Later

| Where | What to click |
|---|---|
| The [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page | **Edit**, under the test's title |
| The test's settings page | **Edit quiz**, under the test's title |
