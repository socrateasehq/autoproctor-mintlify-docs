AutoProctor monitors candidates in real time using on-device AI and captures evidence only when it detects a violation. This means you review flagged incidents rather than hours of footage.

## Data Feeds

AutoProctor can access the following feeds on the candidate's device:

| Feed | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Camera feed** | Monitors the candidate's face and surroundings |
| **Microphone feed** | Detects background noise and audio cues |
| **Screen share feed** | Captures screen activity during the test |
| **Auxiliary device camera feed** | Monitors via a paired secondary device (e.g., phone) |

<Note>
Which feeds AutoProctor accesses depends entirely on how you configure each test. You turn each feed on or off in your [proctoring settings](/proctoring-settings).
</Note>

## What AutoProctor Detects and Records

Unlike traditional proctoring platforms that record full video and audio sessions, AutoProctor detects violations and shows you evidence of only those violations, so you do not spend hours reviewing each candidate's attempt.

The last column is the switch that turns each feature on, in the **Proctoring** section of a test's **Settings**.

| Detection Feature | What It Does | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| **Background audio detection** | Records noise and audio cues from the microphone feed | **Microphone** |
| **Face detection** | Captures photos when no face or multiple faces appear on camera | **Camera** |
| **Tab/app switching** | Captures screenshots when candidates switch tabs or applications | **Tab switching** |
| **Random photos** | Takes photos at random intervals throughout the exam | **Photos at random** |
| **Multiple monitor check** | Blocks the test while an extra monitor is connected to the device | **Prevent multiple monitors** |
| **Multi-session detection** | Detects attempts made from multiple devices or sessions at the same time | **Multi-session detection** |
| **Pre-test face capture** | Takes a photo of the candidate's face before the test starts | **Photo before test start** |
| **Full-screen enforcement** | Ensures the test runs in full-screen mode and flags exits | **Enforce full screen** |
| **Session action recording** | Logs mouse clicks and keyboard activity throughout the test | **Session recording** |
| **Auxiliary device pairing** | Monitors via a paired phone to detect keyboard usage, ensuring candidates are not using ChatGPT or other tools to cheat | **Auxiliary device** |

<Note>
All tracking features are configurable per test. Turn on only what you need in your [proctoring settings](/proctoring-settings).
</Note>

## How to Configure Tracking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your test settings">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Settings** next to the test.

    {/* screenshot: one test row on the Tests page, cropped to the row, with the "Settings" button visible */}

    <Frame caption="A test on the Tests page, with its Settings button on the right">
      <img src="/images/what-gets-tracked-during-proctoring/test-row-settings.png" alt="First row of the Tests page under the column headings Title, Created, Settings, Started and Finished. The test is Chemistry Midterm, Grade 11, with Share and Preview links under its title and Settings and Results buttons at the right" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Proctoring section">
    Choose **Proctoring** from the list of sections, and turn on the switch at the top right of the section. While the switch is **Off**, nothing is monitored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the features you need">
    Under **What gets tracked** and **Enhanced proctoring**, turn each tracking feature on or off depending on your requirements. For example, you might turn on **Camera** and **Tab switching** but leave **Microphone** off.

    {/* screenshot: Proctoring section of Test Settings on a Google Forms test, switch On, cropped to the "What gets tracked" group with "Tab switching" and "Camera" On and "Microphone" Off */}

    <Frame caption="Tab switching and Camera on, Microphone off">
      <img src="/images/what-gets-tracked-during-proctoring/tab-and-camera-on.png" alt="What gets tracked group with nine switches. Tab switching, Camera and Photos at random are on. Microphone and the others are off" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save settings** to apply your changes. These settings take effect immediately for all future test attempts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Enabling more tracking features increases the processing load on the candidate's device. If your candidates use older hardware, consider enabling only the features you need most. See [Device Compatibility](/device-compatibility) for minimum requirements.
</Warning>
