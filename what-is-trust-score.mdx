AutoProctor assigns a Trust Score (0 to 100%) to every proctoring report. The score gives you a quick summary of how likely it is that a candidate maintained test integrity, so you can focus your review time on the attempts that need it most.

</Frame>

A lower Trust Score means AutoProctor detected more suspicious behavior during the test.

<Warning>
Always review the supporting evidence before drawing conclusions. **Do not** rely solely on the Trust Score. Review the actual violation photos, screenshots, and audio recordings to determine whether misconduct occurred.
</Warning>

## How the Trust Score Is Calculated

AutoProctor monitors the camera, microphone and screen, depending on your [proctoring settings](/proctoring-settings). It scores violations on three factors:

| Factor | How It Affects the Score |
|---|---|
| **Type of violation** | Different violations carry different weights. Tab switching impacts the score more heavily than noise detection. |
| **Frequency of violations** | More incidents result in a lower score. |
| **Duration of violations** | Extended violations reduce the score more significantly than brief ones. |

## What Is a Good Trust Score?

As a general guideline, review the evidence for any candidate with a Trust Score **below 85%**. This threshold is meant for review, not as proof of misconduct.

<Note>
Environmental factors can lower the score without any cheating. A candidate in a noisy room near traffic could score 0%, because the microphone cannot always tell speech from background sound.
</Note>

## How to Review a Trust Score

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your test results">
    Go to your [**Tests**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/home/) page and click **Results** next to the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify candidates for review">
    Click the **Trust score** column header to sort the table, and focus on candidates scoring **below 85%**. The **Trust score** filter above the table shows only attempts below a percentage you choose.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/what-is-trust-score/lowest-trust-first.png" alt="Submissions table sorted by Trust score, lowest first" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the violation evidence">
    Click **View report** on a candidate's row to see the flagged photos, screenshots and audio clips. Use them to decide whether the violations are misconduct or false positives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A **Trust score** of **Pending** means the attempt is submitted and its score is still being calculated. A dash means the attempt is not submitted yet, or it was not proctored.
</Note>

<Tip>
To review Trust Scores across all your tests at once, open [**Latest attempts**](https://www.autoproctor.co/test-admin/latest-attempts/). It lists every submitted attempt, newest first, with its **Trust score** and a **View report** link.
</Tip>
